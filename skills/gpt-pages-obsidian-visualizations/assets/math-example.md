# Observation calculations

Inline notation: $E = mc^2,\quad h_i = h_0 + \Delta h_i$.

## Mean water level

$$
\bar{h} = \frac{1}{4}\sum_{i=1}^{4} h_i
= \frac{1.2 + 1.5 + 1.8 + 1.5}{4} = 1.5\,\mathrm{m}
$$

$$

$$
